Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

n4lab is a private tool used only by the owner of the n4lab YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on his own channel through the YouTube Data API. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent (Google OAuth), the app uses YouTube API scopes to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists, and read the status of those uploads on the owner's own channel.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer and in the private, temporary environment that runs the upload. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models. Nothing is collected from viewers.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
